# ActionHub Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to everyone participating in ActionHub project
spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other
maintainer-run channels.

## Expected behavior

- Treat participants with respect, regardless of their background, identity,
  experience, or level of involvement.
- Keep feedback specific, constructive, and focused on the work.
- Respect personal boundaries and ask before sharing someone else's private
  information or communications.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, slurs, unwanted sexual attention, and
sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Retaliation
against anyone who reports a concern or participates in a review is also
unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Contact a project maintainer privately using a contact method on their GitHub
profile. If the report concerns that maintainer, contact another maintainer if
one is available. For incidents on GitHub, you can also report the content or
account through GitHub's reporting tools.

Include a short description, approximate dates, and links or screenshots when
available. Share only the details needed to review the report. Maintainers
should limit disclosure to those who need the information to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports and respond according to the severity and history of
the behavior. They may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
or block accounts from project spaces. Participants may ask for a review by a
maintainer who was outside the original decision, where available.
